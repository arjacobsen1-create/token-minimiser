ⓘWhat this measures, and what it doesn't

Measures: input tokens, counted (exact for GPT models); predicted output tokens by task type; and the predicted total saving once an output cap is attached. Doesn't measure: proven savings (only an actual model response proves one), answer quality, or vendor consumption units such as SAP AI Units, whose conversion rates vendors set per scenario. On a flat subscription the dollar figures are an API-equivalent yardstick for what burns your allowance; on per-token or consumption pricing they are a direct forecast.

Accuracy safeguard: everything on this page is a predicted saving. Input counts are real (exact for GPT models); the output side is estimated from task-type behaviour and the control level you set. A saving is only proven when measured against an actual model response.
